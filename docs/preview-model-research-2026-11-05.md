## What can be settled now

- **Private local modification differs from sharing.** CC BY 4.0 sections 1(i), 2(a) and 3(a) distinguish private reproduction/adaptation from making material public. Its attribution/change conditions apply when sharing. Our extension ships no voice weights, uploads no modified model, and provides no synthesis server. Therefore a user's private instrumented model is not, merely because it was modified, a requirement for us to publish that model. We retain source/license and change information as good provenance. This assessment applies where CC BY actually covers the model; a dataset-only label does not establish that scope. [CC BY legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en).
- **No automatic GPL license for generated speech.** GPLv3 section 2 distinguishes program output from the program: output is covered when its content constitutes a covered work. Merely using the GPL phonemizer does not automatically license every recording under GPL. Input text/model/sample rights still need their own assessment. The exact GPL text is supplied in `vendor/espeak-ng/licenses/COPYING`; see the [GPL](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html) and [FSF output explanation](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html#GPLOutput).
- **Original preview production avoids copying upstream samples.** We can write our own short texts and synthesize recordings with models whose grants support the intended use. This removes reliance on permission to copy/rehost the existing Piper sample recordings or their Wikipedia passages for those replacement files. It does not establish a grant for every model or imply all synthetic output is automatically unrestricted.
- **Individually established model grants.** LJSpeech medium's original trainer declares the model public domain; Kasandra medium has Ondřej Šimek's explicit CC BY 4.0 model grant, matched to our exact checkpoint. The catalog-wide original recording workflow and provenance are in [voice-previews](../voice-previews/README.md). [Trainer grant](https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/), [Kasandra grant](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/discussions/93).

## What the public evidence does not settle

**Lessac descendants:** an existing maintainer discussion answers the question more directly than the old general FAQ. On September 10, 2026, Michael Hansen explained that model-card license fields generally describe the training dataset and left derivative-work scope open. On September 17 he said the project makes no statement about rights arising from the Lessac recordings and directed redistribution questions to those rights holders. This is neither a downstream grant nor a ruling that all fine-tuned models are unlawful. Our files are source-host downloads, not bundled redistribution; that distinction does not establish every intended use is authorized. [Discussion #105](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/discussions/105), [Lessac agreement and its defined Materials](https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/projects/blizzard/2013/lessac_blizzard2013/license.html).

**Retraining is not yet a verified replacement for our pins:** the same discussion first referred to a scratch-trained LibriTTS-R base, then on October 19 changed plans to a single-speaker HiFi-TTS base. The currently published LibriTTS-R card still describes Lessac initialization. A future retraining plan, voice name or permissive dataset label cannot identify the lineage of our pinned file. Match an actual released replacement and its grant before switching. [Current card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/libritts_r/medium/MODEL_CARD), [maintainer update](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/discussions/105).

RHVoice Irina's public repository has no license file, and a separate existing discussion about the same Piper use has no maintainer answer. Another application's license table is not an owner grant. [Lisa creator](https://huggingface.co/wezzmeister/piper-voices/tree/c002346), [Irina source](https://github.com/RHVoice/irina-rus), [existing Irina discussion](https://github.com/RHVoice/RHVoice/discussions/1061).

**Explicit grants need individual reading too:** Alma explicitly licenses its weights CC BY 4.0, unlike dataset-only fields. Bass has an Apache-2.0 model declaration but also states Lessac initialization, so the declaration does not independently settle that ancestry. These are useful individual leads, not blanket clearance. [Alma author/model terms](https://huggingface.co/yeagersthlm/piper-voice-sv-alma), [Bass author/model terms](https://huggingface.co/blackbartblues/piper-pl-bass-high).

**Existing upstream recordings:** the pinned Piper sample repository still has no general audio license. Its player deliberately exposes public playback, but that is not an explicit off-site redistribution license. GitHub's public-content rights are limited to use through its service; they are not a blanket license for independently rehosting files. Locally generating our own recordings does not change or clear the existing streaming integration. [Pinned source](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper-samples/tree/da672d1157136d07365790f51da3357a17cf9650), [GitHub terms D.5](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service#d-user-generated-content).

## Practical route without contacting anyone

1. Produce original texts/recordings for individually substantiated models, with exact model and recording identity, using the existing local speech engine.
2. Host these recordings separately from the extension and stream them in the existing Listen surface. Do not bundle audio, download a model for auditioning or add cloud synthesis.
3. For remaining voices, use existing public author grants or verified compatible replacements. Do not infer permission from a mirror's MIT label, give an unrelated voice's recording as a preview, silently remove voices, or call the unchanged 178-model catalog cleared.

The managed scope is the entire 178-model catalog, including every language and quality variant. Preview production follows a shared catalog-driven workflow, with per-model generation results and license evidence. Public hosting and production integration remain unfinished; catalog/lineage questions remain open. No release was triggered.
